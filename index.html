HOW IT WORKS

Four steps. No sign-up for anyone.

01

Pick a trader

Search any address on Hyperliquid or pick one from the live board. Their real positions and PnL are on their page.

02

Launch the coin

Name it, add an image, launch. One coin per trader, on HyperEVM, priced in HYPE.

03

People trade it

Every coin trades against HYPE on its own curve from the first block. No presale, no team tokens.

04

The trader gets paid

Every trade pays a 1% fee. The trader's share goes to their Hyperliquid spot balance on its own.
